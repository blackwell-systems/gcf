# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**Note:** This is a working template. Have it reviewed by counsel before relying on it, and collect signatures through a mechanism that creates a durable record (a CLA-assistant bot on pull requests, or a signed file). The purpose of this CLA is to keep the project's copyright chain clean so the maintainer retains the right to license the project (including under future or commercial terms). Without it, outside contributions can prevent relicensing.

## How to sign

- **Individuals:** sign via the CLA-assistant check on your first pull request (or add your name, GitHub handle, and date to `CONTRIBUTORS` and state "I have read and agree to the CLA").
- **On behalf of an organization:** contact the Maintainer for a Corporate CLA.

Alternative: the Project may accept the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)** via `Signed-off-by` on commits for lightweight provenance, but the copyright and relicensing grants above are what preserve the Maintainer's licensing flexibility, so the CLA is preferred for non-trivial Contributions.
